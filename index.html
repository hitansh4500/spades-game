♠ Official Spades Rules Reference

1. Starter Determination: After each player enters their bid, the player with the highest announced bid begins and leads the first trick.

2. Fulfilling the Team Contract: If a team takes at least their combined bid in tricks, they score 10 points per bid trick plus 1 point per extra trick (bag).

3. Failed Contract (Going Set): If a team fails to take enough packs to meet their bid, they lose 10 points per bid trick. If this happens in Round 1, scores go into negative.

4. The 10-Bag (Sandbagging) Penalty: For every 10 bags/overtricks a team accumulates over the match, 100 points are deducted from their total score, and 10 bags are cleared.

5. Nil Bids (0 Tricks): A player who bids 0 is betting they will take zero packs. If successful, their team gains +100 bonus points. If they take even 1 trick, their team loses -100 points.

6. Winning Condition: The first team to reach 500 points wins the match! If neither team reaches 500, play continues for unlimited rounds.
